# Brag Plan: Destaka

## What is this app?
Destaka is a Brazilian SaaS that puts your Google My Business profile on autopilot, optimizing listings, writing posts, and responding to reviews so local customers find you first instead of your competitor next door.

## The angle
The product's own copy is razor-sharp: "Seu cliente esta no Google agora, procurando o que voce faz. Ele vai te encontrar?" This is a dark, confident product that speaks directly to the fear of invisibility. The video should feel like a premium tool ad that mirrors the product's own dark teal aesthetic, letting the score reveal be the centerpiece moment.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant question mark moment: "Seu cliente esta no Google agora." White text on dark teal. The sentence that every local business owner feels. Then the pivot: "Ele vai te encontrar?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Score Destaka reveal: a big "43/100" slams in with category breakdown bars (Perfil, Reputacao, Visibilidade, Retencao, Conversao) filling up. The score label reads "Presenca Fraca" in red. This is the diagnostic moment.
- Three stat cards arrive one by one: "46% das buscas sao locais", "76% visitam no mesmo dia", "88% confiam em avaliacoes". These are the product's own stats from the hero section.
- The score transforms: 43 climbs to 87, bars fill up, label shifts to "Presenca Forte" in green. The autopilot promise.

## Outro / punchline
The Destaka logo and pin mark land center screen. Below: "Google Meu Negocio no piloto automatico." The tagline the product already uses. Clean, confident, done.

## User flow worth showing
Diagnostic score reveal (entry) -> see what's broken (key action) -> autopilot fixes it, score rises (result). The centerpiece scenes simulate this flow: low score exposed, stats that prove urgency, score climbing as Destaka works.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium dark-mode SaaS tool, confident and clean
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, generous spacing. Let the score and stats breathe. Confidence through restraint. The teal accent glows, nothing screams.

## Format: landscape -- 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #071a19 (deep dark teal)
- Accent: #14B8A6 (teal), bright variant #5EEAD4
- Text: #ffffff (primary), rgba(255,255,255,0.7) (secondary)
- Display font: Outfit (bold, 400/500/700)
- Body font: Geist Sans
- Strongest visual element: the Score Hero component with big number, progress bars, and faixa badge. The pin-eye SVG logo mark in teal.

## Share copy (draft)
Seu cliente esta no Google agora, procurando o que voce faz. Ele vai te encontrar? Destaka: Google Meu Negocio no piloto automatico.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm professional bed with restrained accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady, clean, fits polished)
- Music treatment: fade in from 0s at 0.30 volume, maintain through highlights, gentle fade-out under logo at ~18s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at ~110 BPM. Strong cues: 8.74s for score slam, 13.11s for third stat card landing, 17.47s for logo reveal. Beat grid for sequential stat cards around 10.93s, 12.02s, 13.11s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make the score number glow and progress bar presence breathe slightly. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, polished. 3-4 sounds total. Motion-matched.
- Audio-coupled moments: score number slam (impact bell), stat cards arriving one by one (soft card/drop sounds), logo landing (gentle announcement cue)
- Restraint rule: no stacking SFX. No aggressive hits. Everything quiet and professional.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 -- Hook -- 4s
Dark #071a19 background. Subtle teal radial glow top-right (matching the site's hero gradient). Text fades in, line by line:
"Seu cliente esta no Google agora."
Then, slightly delayed:
"Ele vai te encontrar?"
The second line in #14B8A6 teal accent. Both in Outfit bold, generous size.
Sequential/interaction: yes, two text lines appear sequentially with a 0.8s gap
Audio intent: quiet tension, the music bed establishes
Audio-coupled idea: subtle text fade-in, no typing -- clean entrance
Music: warm, steady, low
Transition mood: soft -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 -- Score Reveal -- 5s
The diagnostic dashboard appears. Center: a large "43" in red (#EF4444) with "/100" muted beside it. Below, a badge reads "Presenca Fraca". Five thin progress bars fill up to their respective (low) levels: Perfil 12/25, Reputacao 8/25, Visibilidade 6/20, Retencao 10/20, Conversao 7/10. The whole card sits on the dark background with the same card styling as the product (rounded-2xl, subtle border).
Sequential/interaction: yes, the score number slams in first, then bars fill one by one over 1.5s
Audio intent: the weight of the diagnostic truth. One confident impact.
Audio-coupled idea: score number slam on beat, bars fill with subtle progression
Music: building slightly
Transition mood: clean -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 -- Stats -- 5s
Three stat cards appear one by one, left to right. Each card has the teal pin icon, a large stat number, and a short label:
- "46%" / "das buscas sao locais"
- "76%" / "visitam no mesmo dia"
- "88%" / "confiam em avaliacoes"
Cards use the product's own card style: dark bg, teal accent border, pin icon.
Sequential/interaction: yes, three cards arrive one by one, ~1s apart on beat grid
Audio intent: rhythm and urgency building through the stats
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card arrival with soft drop/card-place sounds
Music: steady beat, stats land on grid
Transition mood: clean -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 -- Transformation + Outro -- 6s
The score transforms: "43" counts up to "87". The color shifts from red to green (#4ADE80). Badge changes to "Presenca Forte". Progress bars extend to high values. Hold for 1.5s.
Then crossfade to the Destaka logo (pin-eye mark + "Destaka" wordmark in white) centered. Below: "Google Meu Negocio no piloto automatico." in secondary text color.
Sequential/interaction: yes, score counter animation 43->87 over 1.5s, then logo fade
Audio intent: resolution, confidence, the payoff
Audio-coupled idea: score climb with subtle tick energy, logo lands with gentle bell
Music: fades gently under logo
Transition mood: soft crossfade to end

**Music mood for this video:** steady, clean, professional
**Audio summary:** a warm music bed establishes tone from frame 1, score slam lands on 8.74s strong cue, stat cards tick on beat grid, logo reveals on 17.47s cue, music fades gently to close.

## Music cue guidance
- Track: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (~110 BPM)
- Strong cue targets: 8.74s (score slam in Scene 2), 17.47s (logo reveal in Scene 4)
- Beat-grid windows: stat cards in Scene 3 at ~10.93s, ~12.02s, ~13.11s
- Restraint: polished tone. Use only 2 strong cue locks. Beat-grid for stats only.
